# Related work and data sources

This is a starting reading list, not a systematic review. Claims and implementation details should be checked against full papers, software versions, and dataset documentation before publication.

## Environments and decision methods

- [Rothermel (1972), USDA Forest Service](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/32533): original surface-fire spread model; source for the initial candidate equation.
- [Andrews (2018), USDA Forest Service](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/55928): consolidated Rothermel equations, assumptions, and related spread-direction treatments.
- [Anderson (1982), USDA Forest Service](https://doi.org/10.2737/INT-GTR-122): original 13 fire-behavior fuel models; FM1 short-grass parameters used in the first Rothermel subset.
- [Scott and Burgan (2005), USDA Forest Service](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/9521): 40 standard fire-behavior fuel models, including GR1 and SH1, with dynamic herbaceous fuel treatment.
- [Pyretechnics](https://github.com/pyregence/pyretechnics), pinned at 2026.8.10 for the optional standard-fuel path: implements the fuel catalogue and Rothermel surface-fire calculations; Nightjar supplies its own grid-arrival coupling. EPL-2.0 applies to that dependency.
- [McFayden et al., *Reference Guide to the Drop Effectiveness of Skimmer and Rotary Wing Airtankers* (Canadian Forest Service, 2023)](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2023/rncan-nrcan/Fo123-2-35-2023-eng.pdf): CL-415 capacity, typical indicated airspeeds, drop conditions, footprint measurements, and simplified cycle timing; transfer to Nightjar conditions requires validation.
- [Avincis CL-415 specification](https://www.avincis.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/A4-CL-415-Specifications-Flyer-Low-Res.pdf): operator specification of 6,137 L water-tank capacity.
- The integrated example's 35 m permanent water barrier is an unsourced software-test assumption. None of the aircraft references above supports that footprint or effect.
- [Copernicus EMSR578](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/activations/EMSR578/), [EMSR590](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/activations/EMSR590/), [EMSR592](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/activations/EMSR592/), and [EMSR600](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/activations/EMSR600/): registered CEMS incident products used for development, validation, and reserved static maps. The [CEMS data terms](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/terms-and-conditions/) govern use and attribution; product-level SHA-256 values and acquisition times are recorded in `nightjar/cems_catalog.py` and copied into generated scenario provenance. The EMSR578 source audit and limitations are in [VALIDATION_EMSR578.md](VALIDATION_EMSR578.md). Supplied bases must match their activation keys and source classification; synthetic fixtures carry no CEMS ZIP checksum. The identity regression evidence uses generated fixtures, without new external products. PPO verifies the stage, ZIP digest, product code, filename, and acquisition time against the same catalog entry; these consistency tests do not establish physical validity.

- [SimFire](https://github.com/mitrefireline/simfire) and [SimHarness](https://github.com/mitrefireline/simharness): open wildfire simulation and RL harness. Relevant for environment boundaries, mitigation actions, and baseline design.
- [Cell2Fire](https://github.com/cell2fire/Cell2Fire): cell-based propagation with fuel, weather, moisture, topography, and spatial/statistical outputs. Candidate external comparator for unmitigated spread.
- [Oulu Wildfire Drones](https://github.com/Arritmic/oulu-wildfire-drones): drone-response simulation with PPO, heuristic, auction, refill constraints, and operational metrics. Relevant to the two-aircraft stage.
- [JaxWildfire paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06102): vectorized wildfire simulation for RL. Relevant if measured simulation throughput becomes a bottleneck; the linked [repository](https://github.com/ori-goals/JaxWildfire) did not provide implementation code at the time of review.
- [Vo et al. (2025), task-allocation comparison](https://consensus.app/papers/a-comparative-analysis-of-task-allocation-strategies-for-vo-casado/fc5abcb8184c5d789dafec0d79f9f192/): PPO versus heuristic and auction in resource-constrained drone response.
- [Tapley et al. (2023), SimFire/SimHarness](https://consensus.app/papers/reinforcement-learning-for-wildfire-mitigation-in-tapley-dotter/b5817762f0ac5665b44ba705545b5555/): simulation-based wildfire mitigation research.

## External grass-fire diagnostic

- [Cruz et al. (2024), The 1986 Annaburroo experimental grassland fires: data](https://connectsci.au/wf/article/33/5/WF23100/22126/The-1986-Annaburroo-experimental-grassland-fires): plot variables, experimental context, and definition of the width-adjusted steady-state spread rate.
- [CSIRO Data Access Portal, Annaburroo collection csiro:58746](https://data.csiro.au/collection/csiro:58746): collection version 1 and downloadable data. See [the source audit and diagnostic](VALIDATION_ANNABURROO.md).

- [PyTorch installation guide](https://pytorch.org/get-started/locally/): installation and import checks for macOS and CPU Linux. The geographic PPO verification used PyTorch 2.14.0, Python 3.12.8, and Pyretechnics 2026.8.10 on CPU (2026-09-30); the tested version does not imply compatibility across every `torch>=2.0.0` release. CI resolves the CPU requirement from `https://download.pytorch.org/whl/cpu`.

## Surface-fire experiment sources (reviewed 2026-09-30)

- Butler et al. (2016), [Observations of energy transport and rate of spreads from low-intensity fires](https://doi.org/10.1071/WF14154), IJWF25, 76–89. Tables 1/4 and ignition descriptions constrain interpretation; summarized rates are not complete forcing/front trajectories.
- Jimenez and Butler (2016), [RxCADRE in-situ fire behavior](https://doi.org/10.2737/RDS-2016-0038); [DataLumos mirror E249600V1](https://doi.org/10.3886/E249600V1). Original download HTTP 403; mirror requires ICPSR login. No ZIP acquired.
- Seto and Clements (2015), [CSU-MAPS background weather](https://doi.org/10.2737/RDS-2015-0027); Ottmar and Restaino (2014), [ground fuels](https://doi.org/10.2737/RDS-2014-0028). Source contracts must resolve heights, times, moisture and fuel mapping before empirical prediction.
- Pyretechnics version 2026.8.10, pinned in `requirements-standard.txt`. The numerical comparison uses its `eulerian_level_set` module alongside Nightjar Dijkstra, with identical calm local-rate inputs. The 5%/30%/20% thresholds are project research choices, not thresholds attributed to these references.
- Firemodels, [CSIRO C064/F19 experimental CSVs](https://github.com/firemodels/exp/tree/e5de6811036d252b4f9085866f08da66efdda7d3/CSIRO_Grassland_Fires) and [FDS experiment documentation](https://github.com/firemodels/fds/blob/bb1fb3a6f90a9a7de16ecf436c0092bfbfe852f2/Manuals/FDS_Validation_Guide/Experiment_Chapter.tex), revisions retrieved 2026-09-30. Files downloaded with verified acquisition hashes. The chapter cites Cheney et al. (1993) and Mell et al. (2007) and distinguishes measured grass properties from assumptions. The [executed diagnostic](VALIDATION_CSIRO_HEAD.md) scores the distinct front/head/travel definitions separately; it does not claim independent or full physical validation.

- Mell et al. (2007), [A physics-based approach to modelling grassland fires](https://doi.org/10.1071/WF06002), IJWF16, 1–22; [accessible primary PDF](https://www.frames.gov/documents/catalog/spa/mell_jenkins_gould_cheney_2007a.pdf). Table 1 and pp. 16–18/Figure 16 establish fuel inputs, leading-edge geometry, ignition duration, F19 wind shift and the published Rothermel disagreement. Eq. 2 supplies an in-campaign finite-width grass reference; it is not independent evidence on these burns.
- Cheney, Gould and Catchpole (1998), [Prediction of fire spread in grasslands](https://doi.org/10.1071/WF9980001), IJWF8, 1–13. The complete operational system has wind-height, pasture and curing conventions beyond the restricted Eq. 2 reference implemented here. Annaburroo contributed to the grass-model development; see Cruz et al. (2024).

- Clements et al. (2007), [Observing the dynamics of wildland grass fires: FireFlux](https://doi.org/10.1175/BAMS-88-9-1369), BAMS 88, 1369–1382; [acquired primary PDF](https://research.fs.usda.gov/download/treesearch/14829.pdf). Published fuel/ambient-wind estimates and distinct photo, front-passage and plume endpoints constrain the [executed independent-campaign upper-bound diagnostic](VALIDATION_FIREFLUX.md). Its 9% dead moisture is an estimate, not the 18.5% mixed morning measurement. [Clements' AMS 2007 overview](https://ams.confex.com/ams/7firenortheast/techprogram/paper_127373.htm) gives preliminary source variants, not interval uncertainty bounds.
- Kochanski et al. (2013), [Evaluation of WRF-SFIRE performance with field observations from the FireFlux experiment](https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-6-1109-2013), GMD 6, 1109–1126. Final PDF acquired and pinned; §5.1's literal observed 1.61 m/s remains the declared comparison despite an apparent Figure 4 timing/§2 distance discrepancy. Its tuned WRF-SFIRE parameters and simulated outputs are not adopted as independent observations or Nightjar inputs.
- Costes et al. (2022), [Effects of high-density gradients on wildland fire behavior in coupled atmosphere-fire simulations](https://doi.org/10.1029/2021MS002955), JAMES 14, e2021MS002955; [author-hosted acquired paper](https://www.umr-cnrm.fr/old/IMG/pdf/costes_etal_2022_james.pdf). §5.1 explains filtering and temporal standard deviations. [Zenodo record 6372268](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6372268), 2022-03-21, CC-BY-4.0, supplies acquired filtered main-tower observations and simulation ensembles in the same NPZ. Publisher MD5 and local SHA-256 verified; observation-only auditing excludes simulated ROS/arrivals, unresolved column mappings and pickled failure masks. No raw 20 Hz archive or ST/front chronology is claimed.

## Cheney 1998 reconstruction evidence (accessed 2026-09-30)

- Cheney NP, Gould JS, Catchpole WR (1998), Prediction of Fire Spread in Grasslands, IJWF8,1–13, [DOI 10.1071/WF9980001](https://doi.org/10.1071/WF9980001). Original PDF not acquired; abstract is not equation evidence.
- Cruz et al. (2015), Australian Forestry 78,118–158, [corrected full coauthor restatement](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/281801393_Empirical-based_models_for_predicting_head-fire_rate_of_spread_in_Australian_fuel_types), DOI 10.1080/00049158.2015.1055063, corrigendum 10.1080/00049158.2015.1080789; section 2.1.2 Eqs 6–11. Distinguish original curing 10a from updated 10b.
- Cruz, Alexander, Kilinc (2022), [Fire 5,55](https://doi.org/10.3390/fire5020055), primary wildfire study AppendixA.3. Downloaded PDF SHA-256 `3ecfa6cd2329412c898a3e61bc57dd895c9e605310254c9c80c6a9edaf3ef08d`. A4 prints 0.705 rather than the 2015 restatement's 0.715; A7 uses later curing. Neither is silently imported.
- Wotton, Alexander, Taylor (2009), [GLC-X-10](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2010/nrcan/Fo123-2-10-2009-eng.pdf), section 3.2.2 p 9, original Australian curing curve: independent restatement of the original Australian curing curve within development of another Canadian function. Read PDF; no locally acquired-byte hash claimed.
- Cruz et al. (2015), [primary curing experiment](https://doi.org/10.1071/WF14146), IJWF24,838–848, supports limits of original curing assumptions; later fitted curve excluded.
- Gould, Cruz, Sullivan (2024), [original Annaburroo data article](https://doi.org/10.1071/WF23100), Table 1: `10mWind.ms` is profile-extrapolated open wind; E2/E3 cut treatments and T1 undisturbed.
- CSIRO 2017 [fuel-load experiment report](https://doi.org/10.13140/RG.2.2.28866.68804), section 3.1.4, and Cruz et al. (2018), [DOI 10.1071/WF18082](https://doi.org/10.1071/WF18082), Appendix: Australian U 2/U 10 conversion. This is sensitivity provenance, not a validated FireFlux conversion.

See [the protocol](CHENEY_1998_PROTOCOL.md) for exact equations, source versions, domains, equality conventions and unresolved original-paper reconciliation.

## Follow-up grass evidence and numerical verification (2026-09-30)

- Cruz et al. (2018), [Got to burn to learn: the effect of fuel load on grassland fire behaviour and its management implications](https://doi.org/10.1071/WF18082), [full author-uploaded text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/327982732_Got_to_burn_to_learn_the_effect_of_fuel_load_on_grassland_fire_behaviour_and_its_management_implications). Six-site 58-burn experiment, fully cured plots, documented U2/U10 profile, weather-estimated moisture and operational equations. Published aggregate MAE/mean signed error are not a Nightjar reproduction; matched individual input rows were not acquired.
- Cruz, Alexander, Kilinc (2022), [Fire 5,55](https://doi.org/10.3390/fire5020055), methods/Data Availability: mixed original-paper/later-run provenance and per-run data under request. The 58 retained runs cannot all be declared independent of 1998 development.
- Dabrowski et al., [arXiv:2212.00970v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.00970), 2023 author preprint, sections 4.2.1–4.2.2: Braidwood wind measured at 2 m, finite-head development and initial acceleration; [public implementation](https://github.com/jjdabr/BPINN-Wildfire). Acquired PDF hash and missing empirical inputs are recorded in [the audit](GRASS_INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE.md).
- Athanasiou (2021), [primary Mediterranean grass-fire conference paper](https://eclass.duth.gr/modules/document/file.php/425447/%CE%A5%CE%BB%CE%B9%CE%BA%CF%8C%20%CE%B5%CF%81%CE%B3%CE%B1%CF%83%CF%84%CE%B7%CF%81%CE%AF%CE%BF%CF%85/MiltiadisAthanasiou_Grassfires_ROS.pdf): 20 observations, midflame wind and descriptive summaries; acquired bytes do not provide matched U10/moisture/curing per burn.
- Original Cheney 1998 PDF route from [Crossref metadata](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1071/WF9980001); direct HTTP 403 and publisher browser paywall confirmed. The original full equations remain unreconciled.
- Installed Pyretechnics 2026.8.10 `burn_cells`, `surface_fire`, `conversion`, `fuel_models`, `space_time_cube` and `eulerian_level_set` input/unit conventions. The [extended protocol](PROPAGATION_EXTENDED_PROTOCOL.md) and frozen report hash both readable source files and loaded binaries. Independent spatial geometry shares these input-only constitutive rates; no empirical validation of Rothermel is inferred.
