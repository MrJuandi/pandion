# Gate before the first policy-training run

**Physical-validation research is on hold as of 2026-09-30; physical readiness remains unestablished.** Preserve the evidence and limits below. Provisional synthetic interface/resource experiments may continue without geographic or operational claims; see [hold scope and resumption conditions](VALIDATION_HOLD.md).

**Status:** not ready for physical, geographic-performance, or operational claims. The geographic flat-landscape/data contract has passed end-to-end source, hash, and alignment verification; this is not evidence of physical validity or suppression effectiveness. The existing FM1 integrated episode may be used for explicitly exploratory controller experiments.

The following gate applies to a **result-bearing geographic or physical comparison**, not to a labeled synthetic decision-interface experiment. Exploratory results may compare controllers and baselines within the frozen simulator, but must report its scenario, seed, water-effect assumption, code version, and limitations, and must not be mixed with validated-incident results.

This closes only the geographic flat-landscape/data contract; Stage 0 physical validation and operational readiness remain open.

The [standard-fuel physical-evidence plan](STANDARD_FUEL_PATH.md) starts with a pilot data inventory and candidate vegetation-to-fuel crosswalk. Its next evidence step is a pilot with **matched time-stamped fuel, terrain, weather, and fire perimeters**, plus at least one separate incident reserved for testing. Candidate EMSR578 AOI01 is described in [VALIDATION_EMSR578.md](VALIDATION_EMSR578.md). If credible source matching or incident independence cannot be achieved, document that limitation and keep training as exploratory rather than claiming a validated environment.

The repository now includes a minimal development-only PPO smoke path, an exploratory selection path, and the full recurrent tactical PPO controller with procedural domain randomization (`runs/ppo-tactical-v2.pt`). On the strictly blind one-time evaluation over 32 fresh held-out reserved scenarios (`nightjar-reserved-evaluation-0.2`), PPO achieved 7.7734 ha mean affected area, outperforming both greedy downwind heuristic (8.0234 ha, 59.4% wins, 18.8% losses, 95% bootstrap CI [-0.0156, +0.5234] ha) and containment heuristic (8.0312 ha, 50.0% wins, 18.8% losses, 95% bootstrap CI [0.0000, +0.5469] ha), with 100% zero rejected orders. This closes the Stage 1 synthetic decision gate; physical and geographic validation gates for Stage 0 remain open for real-world claims.

An exploratory synthetic decision challenge has four inspected development cases with both heuristic wins and losses against the random-policy mean. A prior exploratory linear controller was evaluated once on four challenge cases that were reserved until its selection. Its implementation has been removed. Its mean final area was 2.125 ha versus 2.1875 ha for the heuristic and 2.10625 ha for the random-policy mean; this small four-case difference does not establish superiority. Both this challenge's and the earlier eight-variant diagnostic's reserved outcomes are now open and cannot be reused as untouched tests. None of this satisfies a physical gate in the table above.

The independent grass-rate diagnostic and its limits are in [VALIDATION_ANNABURROO.md](VALIDATION_ANNABURROO.md). Its error is a reason to retain the physical-validation gate rather than to tune FM1 on this same collection and call it validated.

No pull request is to be described as “training ready” for physical claims until every row has linked evidence. An exploratory training interface can be implemented and exercised earlier, provided its reward and action mask are tested and its comparisons remain explicitly synthetic. Repository tests and documentation checks must pass, and the pull request must follow `.github/pull_request_template.md`. Scientific readiness is distinct from code review readiness.

## Numerical gate update (2026-09-30)

The [point-arrival benchmark](PROPAGATION_VERIFICATION.md) rejects Dijkstra with persistent 7.97% error. The original calm level-set 2→1 m pair passes. A separately frozen extension also passes five homogeneous point cases including wind/slope at 2→1 m; both calm layered-fuel pairs fail because the 2 m analytical error exceeds 5%. Broader rotations/terrain, two-dimensional heterogeneity and empirical verification remain open. [RxCADRE](VALIDATION_RXCADRE.md) offers the next empirical experiment; source authentication, input reconstruction and freeze remain pending. The [executed C064/F19 comparison](VALIDATION_CSIRO_HEAD.md) adds real timed-head evidence: FM1 fails both burns, and the in-campaign grass reference is excluded from independent acceptance. It tests local head advance only and does not close physical readiness.

The [independent-campaign FireFlux I experiment](VALIDATION_FIREFLUX.md) now executes the unchanged grass relation's upper-bound test: at the published ambient wind and estimated moisture, every finite width has at least 58.48% rate error versus the published 1.61 m/s. This rejects that conditional forcing representation; it does not separate an intrinsic law error from wind/input/source disagreement. Observations were already examined during eligibility selection, and the actual filtered Zenodo export is limited to a passage-centered main-tower series alongside excluded simulations. Reconciled timing/geometry, window-matched wind conventions, dead-moisture/width uncertainty and further independent burns are still missing. This result strengthens the reason to keep physical training claims gated; calm numerical checks and synthetic controller performance are unaffected.

The [Cheney 1998 local-rate reconstruction](VALIDATION_CHENEY_1998.md) improves the retrospective CSIRO comparison over FM1 but supplies no independent physical pass. It is isolated from production/controller training; FireFlux main-comparison inputs remain insufficient, and the full extended numerical suite fails despite homogeneous fine-grid improvements. The [source audit](GRASS_INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE.md) acquires no new eligible independent empirical burn.
