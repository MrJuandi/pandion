Display conventions

  • Terrain may be exaggerated vertically (the "Terrain" selector); the active exaggeration is always shown in the top panel. The physics uses unexaggerated elevation.
  • The 3D surface interpolates cell-corner heights (Catmull-Rom); it adds no physical resolution.
  • Active front: cells reached that have at least one fuel neighbor not yet reached, according to the simulator's arrival raster.
  • The aircraft follows the recorded horizontal positions, linearly interpolated between samples. Its altitude and size are display only and do not come from the replay.
  • The water curtain and smoke are visual effects; the smoke is not simulated.
